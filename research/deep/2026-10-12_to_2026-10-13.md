### Direct code findings (main as inspected at start of run)
1. **[B]** `clsplats/lifter/depth_anything_lifter.py`: per camera, `visible_views[g]` increments once if g appears in *positive* kNN and again if it appears in *negative* kNN. Thus `visible_views[g]` can be 2 for **one camera** and up to `2 * valid_views`, despite the name. `positive_views[g]` increments at most once per positive-supported camera. `positive_ratio = positive_views / visible_views` is not a fraction of physically visible cameras, and can be artificially halved. `min_visible_views=2` can be satisfied from just one camera's positive+negative sampled pixels. `min_positive_views=2` still requires two positive-supported cameras for final retention. No independent projection-based visibility test appears in this evidence accumulator.
2. **[B]** Positive neighbors require local-scale distance AND `|z_gaussian-z_pixel| < depth_tol_abs + depth_tol_rel*d_pixel`; negative neighbors require only local-scale distance. This asymmetric depth gate can attach negative evidence to occluded/depth-inconsistent Gaussians. Impact on active-mask recall remains **unmeasured**.
3. **[B]** `estimate_depth` uses `self._pipe(pil_img)["depth"]` (PIL visualization), converts to float and divides by 255. The official Hugging Face depth-estimation pipeline supplies a distinct `predicted_depth` tensor and explicitly normalizes/quantizes its `depth` visualization to uint8; this means production lifter throws away float precision and introduces per-image 8-bit quantization **before metric disparity alignment**. Official source: https://github.com/huggingface/transformers/blob/main/src/transformers/pipelines/depth_estimation.py and https://github.com/huggingface/transformers/blob/main/docs/source/en/tasks/monocular_depth_estimation.md . An affine alignment can absorb image-wise scale/offset, but cannot undo quantization. **Potentially important additional bottleneck**.
4. **[B]** HF `pipeline("depth-estimation", model=...)` is created with no explicit `device`. Hugging Face pipeline docs say default device is CPU (-1) in supported versions, but actual installed runtime/device must be measured before claiming it runs on CPU. DINO detector separately uses CUDA when available. This could be a substantial latency bottleneck if depth inference defaults to CPU.
5. **[B]** Each valid view samples up to 2,048 positive and 1,024 negative pixels and runs dense `torch.cdist` against *all* N Gaussians. Complexity is O((M_pos+M_neg) N) time and intermediate memory; for N=500,000 and float32, the positive distance matrix alone is 2048*500000*4 = 4.096 GB (decimal) and negative 2.048 GB, before kNN/other allocations. A 10-second production pipeline needs a spatial index, candidate projection, or chunked/streaming kNN.
6. **[B]** Alignment regresses `1/rendered_depth` on normalized mono values with least squares, requires >=200 unchanged anchors and R²>=0.3; otherwise discards the entire camera. No robust fit, uncertainty weighting, or anchor spatial coverage test. Need log `failure_reason`, `anchor_pixels`, R², and `scale_a` by view.
7. **[B]** `trainer.update_cameras` uses `Random(seed).sample` for sparse views; view selection is not geometry-aware. `train.num_change_views` controls both change evidence and subsequent optimization view set, so changing view count simultaneously changes multiple variables.

### Mathematical interpretation / paper linkage
- **[D]** Existing ratio: `r_g = n_pos(g) / [n_pos_supported(g) + n_neg_supported(g)]`, not `n_pos(g)/n_physically_visible(g)`. One camera can contribute to both denominator terms. Proposed correction (NOT yet implemented): `V_g = {v | projected g in image, z>0, visible under alpha/depth occlusion test}`, `P_g = {v in V_g | positive evidence passes symmetric geometry gate}`, then `r_g = |P_g| / max(1, |V_g|)`. Keep positive and negative support as independent confidence values; do not confuse sampled evidence with visibility.
- **[C]** GS-DIFF, *From Pixels to Primitives* (2026), explicitly models anisotropic primitive drift and camera-geometry observability; however its main method compares **two reconstructed Gaussian sets**, whereas our task has one old set plus <=10 RGB. Transfer observability/anisotropic association ideas, not the entire algorithm. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07203
- **[C]** *Mastering Regional 3DGS* calibrates disparity via robust median + mean absolute deviation, `a=s(delta_GS)/s(delta_mono)`, `b=median(delta_GS)-a median(delta_mono)`, and initializes new Gaussians from calibrated depth (Eq. 6–7). Our immediate comparison should be float-depth + robust alignment, **not** blindly transplant its edited/unedited depth-ratio formula to physically changed scenes. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11263-026-02941-w

## 19:00–20:00 — Convergence causal audit: optimization vs topology vs camera exposure

**Research question:** Did the earlier 5-view changed-region PSNR improvement from 100 to 500 steps occur because densification began, or because existing parameters had more optimization/exposure? What else changes before the first new Gaussian can be born?

### Direct code facts and upstream gsplat source
1. **[B]** `configs/cl-splats.yaml` sets `densify_from_iter=500`, `densification_interval=100`, `densify_until_iter=15000`. `CLGaussians._build_strategy` passes these as `refine_start_iter`, `refine_every`, `refine_stop_iter`. `CLSplatsTrainer._train_step` passes **zero-based** `self._timestep_iter` into `gsplat.DefaultStrategy.step_post_backward`. The upstream strategy condition is **`step > refine_start_iter and step % refine_every == 0`**, not `step >= refine_start_iter`. Hence with current defaults, the **first possible duplication/splitting is on zero-based step 600, i.e., the 601st optimization iteration**, subject to actual gradients and version matching. At 100, 500, and even 600 training iterations, default strategy cannot have *grown* Gaussians. Primary upstream source: https://docs.gsplat.studio/main/_modules/gsplat/strategy/default.html (lines ~246–290). **This falsifies our earlier casual explanation that the 100→500 quality improvement was triggered by densification at 500.**
2. **[B]** No explicit ADD-specific Gaussian insertion appears in `prepare_timestep`: change masks are rendered and detected, `lifter.lift` selects existing rows as active, then the active subset is optimized. The only ordinary Gaussian birth is gsplat duplication/splitting at eligible refinement steps. Thus an entirely empty changed-space region has no new primitive center before the first growth, except by moving existing Gaussians; even at the first growth, children inherit locations near existing parents rather than a depth-lifted new-object surface. **Representation support is a plausible limitation, but not an explanation for the measured 100→500 difference by itself.**
3. **[B]** Topology can nevertheless **shrink before iteration 601**. `_constraint_prune_step` executes every `prune_every=50` (when active Gaussians and fitted primitives exist); even with `constraints.lambda_bound=0.0`, low-opacity **active** Gaussians below `densify_prune_opa=0.005` may be hard-pruned. Therefore 'no topology changes at all under 500' would be false; only 'no gsplat-driven Gaussian birth under 600' is supported. Its geometry-outlier hysteresis additionally requires `prune_consecutive=3`. Record births and prunes separately, not just net Gaussian count.
4. **[B]** `_build_strategy(cl_phase=True)` sets `prune_opa=0` and `reset_every=2**31-1` to protect frozen Gaussians. Upstream scale-based global pruning is gated by `step > reset_every`, so for normal update lengths the upstream global prune is not triggered. Earlier concern about inactive rows being removed by default strategy does **not** hold for the inspected CL phase under normal iteration counts. Do not report it as a bug.
5. **[B]** `_pop_random_train_camera` samples without replacement until the view stack empties, then refills. Consequently with V selected cameras and I steps, each view is exposed either `floor(I/V)` or `ceil(I/V)` times, average `E=I/V`. For V=5: I=100→20 exposures/view, I=500→100; for V=50: I=100→2. Comparing different view counts at the same I also changes per-view exposure. Random ordering remains a source of run-to-run variance.
6. **[B]** The `_train_step` PSNR/SSIM are computed from the **pre-update** `rendered` tensor after optimizer step (detached), not from a fresh post-update render. This gives a one-step lag and is training-view, not held-out, quality. Final held-out evaluation is performed separately when diagnostics and test cameras are enabled. Convergence conclusions must prioritize fixed held-out changed-region metrics.
7. **[B]** Default `lambda_bound=0`, so fitted primitives do not exert a geometric optimization constraint by default, although they can still be used by hard pruning. Also, gsplat strategy invokes `torch.cuda.empty_cache()` on refinement steps; if early refinement is enabled, instrument per-step latency spikes. The entire scene is still rendered each optimization step and dense Adam tensors exist for all Gaussians despite gradient masks: optimization latency need not scale with active count.

### Target-paper mechanism: Mastering Regional 3DGS
- **[C]** The paper's Sect. 4.2/Eqs. (6–7) calibrate mono disparity using robust median/mean absolute deviation and seed Gaussians by unprojecting edited pixels with a depth ratio between edited and unedited estimates. The reported initialization ablation shows visibly improved edits after ~300 steps versus up to ~1000 without initialization; **these are editing results, not a measured guarantee for our 10 s physical scene update**. Primary: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11263-026-02941-w .
- **[D]** Proposed independent implementation for ADD: with known camera intrinsics K, pose T_wc, and trustworthy new-depth z_new(u), seed 3D centers `X_new(u)=T_wc[z_new(u) K^{-1}(u_x,u_y,1)^T]` only where change confidence is high; set initial scale from local projected pixel footprint / depth uncertainty, opacity conservatively, and color from observed RGB. Avoid seeding on unverified depth edges or hallucinated back surfaces. This is a **derived design**, not the paper's exact full editing pipeline.
- **[D]** When geometry is novel and old GS has no corresponding surface, the paper's depth ratio may be invalid at disocclusion/reflective areas; compare (i) direct robust calibrated new depth and (ii) before/after depth ratio with explicit unchanged-region anchors. Reject seeds with cross-view reprojection inconsistency.
